GURU SUJATHA'S CONSERVATORY METHOD

Under the direct mastership of Smt. A. Sujatha Balasubramaniyam, we treat classical Bharathanatyam as a rigorous discipline combining physical symmetry, footwork metrics, tala rhythm cycles, and emotional Abhinaya immersion.

ARTISTIC DIRECTOR: A. Sujatha Balasubramaniyam
STYLE TRADITION: Kalakshetra Restructured
FOCUS: Nritta, Abhinaya & Nattuvangam
01.1 // NRITTA BASE

ADAVUS & TALA

Structured mastery of Tatta, Natta, Kuditta Mettu, Mai Adavu, and Sarikkal executed across 3 rhythmic speeds (Kalam).

01.2 // REPERTOIRE

ALARIPPU & JATHISWARAM

Invocation to pure geometric body alignment synchronized with sollukattu rhythmic syllables and swara patterns.

01.3 // EXPRESSION

SHABDAM & VARNAM

The centerpiece of Margam: intricate Jathis interspersed with deep, nuanced facial expression (Bhava & Rasa).

01.4 // FINALE

TILLANA & SLOKAM

Climactic rhythmic velocity celebrating sculpturesque poses, ending in serene devotional shlokas.
